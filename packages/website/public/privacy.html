Summary of Our Privacy Principles

Kloak was built from the ground up on the principle of absolute local-first security. We do not operate user accounts, we do not host a cloud database for your passwords, we do not run telemetry or analytics, and we will never monetize, track, or sell your personal information.

🔒 100% On-Device Vault All passwords, 2FA secrets, and personal records are encrypted with AES-256-GCM directly on your machine.
🚫 No Remote Code Execution The Chrome extension executes only statically bundled local code. It never loads scripts or libraries from remote CDNs.
🌐 Zero Remote Tracking We do not collect IP addresses, browsing histories, page content, device identifiers, or analytics telemetry.
💻 Local IPC Only The browser extension communicates with your local desktop application via local native messaging and loopback (127.0.0.1).

1 Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all software and services provided under the Kloak project, including:

  • Kloak Password Manager (Native macOS Application);
  • Kloak Password Manager Extension (Manifest V3 Google Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Chromium browser extension published on the Chrome Web Store);
  • Kloak Raycast Extension (macOS command-bar extension);
  • The Kloak open-source software repository and static web properties.

2 Information We Handle (And Where It Is Stored)

Kloak handles sensitive authentication data only as strictly necessary to fulfill your password management requests. All data stays under your physical control:

  • Vault Credentials & Secrets: Your account usernames, passwords, credit card numbers, personal identity records, secure notes, and Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) 2FA secrets are encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM. Decryption keys are derived using Argon2id/PBKDF2 and protected by the Apple Secure Enclave / macOS Keychain. They are stored locally on your device's filesystem and are never uploaded to our servers or any cloud provider.
  • Website URLs & Hostnames: When you browse the web, the extension checks the active tab's domain name purely in ephemeral local memory to identify matching credentials in your vault and alert you if the active site is an unauthenticated phishing or lookalike domain. Your browsing history is never recorded, logged, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Extension Settings & Preferences: Non-sensitive UI preferences (such as auto-lock timeout duration, active theme, and display preferences) are stored locally in your browser's sandboxed storage (chrome.storage.local).

3 Browser Permissions & Technical Justification

In accordance with Google Chrome Web Store policies, here is an explicit, transparent justification for every browser permission declared in our Manifest V3 configuration:

Permission Purpose in Kloak Privacy & Security Safeguard
<all_urls> (host_permissions) Enables the autofill content script to detect username, password, and 2FA input fields across arbitrary websites visited by the user. Content scripts run isolated in the browser DOM. No webpage content, text, or inputs are read or transmitted unless the user explicitly triggers autofill on that specific page.
tabs Reads the active tab's domain name to search your local vault for corresponding login credentials and verify domain legitimacy against phishing attempts. Only the domain hostname is inspected in local memory. Browsing history is never stored, logged, or sent over any network.
activeTab Grants temporary script execution authority to insert credentials when the user clicks the autofill button or popup action. Restricted to explicit user interaction. Cannot activate silently in the background.
nativeMessaging Connects the browser extension to the local Kloak desktop daemon (app.kloak.native) running on your Mac. Operates exclusively via local OS pipes/stdin-stdout. No internet connection is created.
sidePanel Allows users to open and view their vault records in Chrome's native Side Panel alongside web pages. The side panel is rendered entirely offline from local extension assets.
clipboardWrite Allows users to copy generated passwords, usernames, and 2FA TOTP codes to their system clipboard. Write-only access; the extension cannot read or inspect preexisting clipboard data.
storage Saves extension UI settings, lock timers, and display modes. Uses isolated, local browser storage (chrome.storage.local) without remote cloud sync.

4 Absolute Ban on Remote Code & Telemetry

In strict compliance with Google Chrome Web Store policies:

  • No Remotely Hosted Code: Kloak does not download, load, or execute any remotely hosted JavaScript, WebAssembly, or scripts. 100% of the extension's code and dependencies are statically bundled into the extension package at build time.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not embed Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, Meta Pixels, or any third-party telemetry tools in the extension or desktop app.
  • No Commercial Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, license, or exchange user data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties under any circumstances.

5 Read-Only Threat Intelligence Lookups

To protect users from fraudulent lookalike domains and brand impersonation attacks, Kloak includes an active phishing engine. When visiting a previously unverified website, Kloak may perform:

  • A read-only public DNS query (via Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS) to verify domain records;
  • A public RDAP query to inspect domain registration age (as newly registered domains carry a higher risk of phishing).

These lookups query only public domain metadata. They never transmit your IP address, username, password, identity, or any credential data.

6 User Control, Export & Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and absolute ownership of your data at all times:

  • Export: You can export your unencrypted or encrypted vault at any time in standardized formats (CSV, JSON).
  • Deletion: You can purge individual items, clear your trash, or wipe your entire vault with 1 click in the Kloak settings menu.
  • Uninstallation: Removing the Kloak desktop application and Chrome extension completely erases all associated data and application state from your computer.

7 Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & COPPA)

Because Kloak operates on a zero-knowledge, local-first architecture:

  • GDPR (EU): We do not act as a data controller or data processor of your personal data on remote servers. All processing occurs on your own hardware under your sole authority.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California): We do not "sell" or "share" your personal information, as defined under California law.
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA): Kloak does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
